# Monster Batch Notes

## Prompt-Safe Art Guidance

Use original creature language throughout. Describe body plan, palette, pose, texture, and mood instead of comparing a line to known games, shows, characters, artists, studios, or mascots.

For easy-to-fail lines, keep the prompt cute, collectible, and toy-like. Favor words like "spark," "glow," "ribbon," "crest," "tuft," "marking," "storm pattern," "soft energy," and "card art." Avoid brand names, direct style claims, real-person likenesses, copied costumes, logo-like markings, and intense harm language.

`bolt` needs extra care because yellow electric cat prompts can drift toward familiar mascot territory. Safer direction: a lemon-yellow storm lynx with graphite paws, blue spark ribbons, long zigzag ear tufts, a tuning-fork tail, cloud-hop pose, and bright curious eyes. Keep the silhouette feline and athletic, not round mascot-shaped. Use "storm lynx," "charge cat," or "spark acrobat"; avoid describing it as an electric mouse, famous game creature, or clone.

Other fragile lines:

- `flame`: lean into candle tails, lantern rings, soot socks, wax-seal brow marks, and festival magician poses. Avoid folklore-copy counts or existing fire-fox mascot comparisons.
- `shadow`: keep it moonlit and playful with crescent patches, smoke-ribbon tails, and soft violet glow. Avoid villain, demon, horror, or scary-real language.
- `ice`: use crystal plates, frost mane, snow-glass fins, and calm guardian posture. Avoid sharp injury framing.
- `toxic`: make it a bright pond alchemist with bubbles, spots, and gooey comedy. Avoid sickness, body-fluid, or medical wording.
- `ghost` / `dark`: use sheet folds, lantern glow, sleepy mansion charm, eclipse patterns, and gentle mystery. Avoid frightening, occult, or realistic haunting cues.
- `storm` / `cloud` / `ramcloud`: use plush cloud wool, spiral horns, breeze scarves, cyclone hooves, and sun-gold storm markings. Avoid disaster wording; keep weather effects decorative.

Batch prompt template:

`Original cute anime collectible creature, [line-safe body plan], [signature shape], [palette], [friendly expression], [dynamic but harmless pose], clean cel shading, sticker-clear silhouette, premium fantasy card art, no logos, no text.`
